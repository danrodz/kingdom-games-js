📜 Game Rules

🎮 Gameplay

  • Players start at random positions on the map
  • Each turn, move 1-3 tiles horizontally or vertically
  • Players cannot pass through obstacles or other players
  • Battle begins when players are adjacent to each other

⚔️ Items on the Map

🗡️ Weapons (4)
  • Dagger: 20 damage
  • Hammer: 22 damage
  • Quiver: 24 damage
  • Sword: 26 damage
🧪 Potions (2)
  • Restores +30 health
  • Available when health is below 50%
🚧 Obstacles (15)
  • Cannot be passed through
  • Block movement paths

⚔️ Combat

  • Initial damage: 10 points
  • Weapon damage replaces initial damage
  • Attack: Deal full damage and receive counter-attack
  • Defend: Take 50% less damage but lose your turn
  • Game ends when a player's health reaches 0
